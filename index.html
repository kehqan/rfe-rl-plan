01

Concentrate on FB + WhatsApp

The two platforms where Afghans actually live. Facebook for mass reach (6.8M combined). WhatsApp for daily distribution and habit (▲200%+, 3× larger than YT+IG combined). Everything else either serves these two or moves to maintenance.

02

05 — Cross-Promotion Funnel

One organic funnel that connects every product to every platform.

Two shifts in one. Original creative instead of link-and-repost — every post built for the platform it sits on. One organic funnel instead of treating each platform as independent — each product feeds the next, with WhatsApp as the conversion + retention layer. No paid promotion.

★ The funnel

From discovery to habit, organically — across both languages.

One asset, six placements — designed so the audience moves toward the highest-engagement surface (WhatsApp) without ever feeling pushed.

01Discovery

Facebook + YouTube + Google

3-min Highlights and YouTube Shorts catch the first-time viewer. Hook in the first 3 seconds, headline within 8 seconds. Subtitle styling that reads with sound off.

02Context

Web + Instagram

Web article (linked from FB + smarturl on WhatsApp) adds depth and audio. IG carousels expand the story into visual context — voxpop strand lives here. Web app gives audio a permanent home.

03Habit

WhatsApp + CDR loop

WhatsApp Audio Reports + voice notes + CDR call-back close the loop. The audience that arrives on FB ends up subscribed where engagement is highest. Telephone-number posts keep the relationship two-way.

★ Example flow

One 3-min Highlight, six placements.

The cross-promotion logic for a single news item — applied identically in Dari and Pashto, no extra production hours.

  1. Web article — the original reporting with photo / audio / contributor quote.
  2. 3-min Highlight — restructured video on Facebook (Reel) + YouTube + IG. Hook in 3 seconds, link to Web in the description.
  3. WhatsApp voice note + smarturl — same story as 30s audio + link to the article. Posted in the daily WA flow.
  4. WhatsApp Audio Report — longer voice piece on the same day (1–2 min) for users who want depth without leaving WA.
  5. IG carousel / voxpop — visual recap with audience reactions (CDR voxpop quote, region tag).
  6. Interview / Debate hook — Wed / Thu episode picks the topic up if it has legs. Clips back to FB + IG + WA.

From the Service's own priority list: train colleagues in video and digital, with practical focus on Reels editing, vertical storytelling, IG carousel design, voice-note production for WhatsApp, and YouTube chaptering / thumbnails.
