Open this tool in one click, from anywhere

Instead of searching for this page every morning, you can add a shortcut to your browser's bookmark bar. Once it's there, one click opens this briefing tool and Copilot Chat side by side — from any page you're on.

1
Make sure your bookmark bar is visible. In Chrome or Edge: press Ctrl+Shift+B (Windows) or ⌘+Shift+B (Mac). A bar with your saved bookmarks will appear below the address bar.
2
Drag the black button below into that bookmark bar — the same way you'd drag a file into a folder. Drop it anywhere in the bar. It will appear as a small shortcut called "Morning Briefing".
3
That's it. Every morning, click it from wherever you are — Outlook, Teams, any webpage — and it will open this briefing tool with your saved settings, ready to go.
